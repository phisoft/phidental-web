Braces

Dental braces are orthodontic appliances designed to straighten and improve the aesthetics and alignment of your teeth. They work by applying gentle, consistent pressure to gradually move teeth into their proper positions, correcting a wide range of dental issues including crowding, spacing, overbites, underbites, and crossbites.

Types of Braces

  • Metal braces – Traditional stainless steel brackets with metal wires; the most common and cost-effective option.
  • Ceramic braces – Tooth-coloured or clear brackets that blend with your teeth for a more discreet appearance.
  • Self-ligating braces – Use a specialised clip instead of elastic bands to hold the wire, reducing friction and treatment time.
  • Lingual braces – Custom-made brackets placed on the back (lingual side) of teeth, completely hidden from view.

Treatment Process

  • Consultation & assessment including X-rays and impressions of your teeth.
  • Fitting of braces – brackets are bonded to teeth and archwires are inserted.
  • Regular adjustments every 4-6 weeks to progressively align teeth.
  • Retention phase after braces removal using retainers to maintain results.

Benefits

  • Improved oral health with better access for cleaning straight teeth.
  • Better chewing function and clearer speech.
  • Enhanced facial aesthetics and a confident smile.
  • Increased self-confidence and overall quality of life.

Dentures

Dentures are custom-made removable appliances designed to replace missing teeth and restore both function and aesthetics. Whether you need to replace a few teeth or an entire arch, modern dentures provide a natural-looking and comfortable solution.

Types of Dentures

  • Complete dentures – Full arch replacement for patients who have lost all teeth in either the upper or lower jaw.
  • Partial dentures – Replace one or more missing teeth while clasping onto remaining natural teeth.
  • Immediate dentures – Placed immediately after tooth extraction, allowing you to have teeth during the healing period.
  • Implant-supported dentures – Snap onto dental implants for superior stability and retention.

Benefits

  • Restored ability to eat and speak properly with confidence.
  • Improved facial appearance by supporting lips and cheeks.
  • Prevents sagging of facial muscles and provides structure.
  • Affordable tooth replacement option compared to alternatives.

Dental Implant

Implant dentistry is a specialised field for the replacement of missing teeth. It involves the surgical placement of titanium implants into the jawbone, which act as artificial tooth roots, providing a strong and permanent foundation for replacement teeth.

Types of Implant Solutions

  • Single tooth implant – Replaces a single missing tooth with a crown attached to the implant.
  • Multiple tooth implant – Replaces several missing teeth using multiple implants and a bridge.
  • All-on-4 / All-on-6 – Full arch restoration using 4 or 6 strategically placed implants to support a complete set of teeth.

Procedure

  • Initial consultation with X-ray and 3D imaging to assess bone density.
  • Implant placement surgery – titanium post is surgically inserted into the jawbone.
  • Osseointegration period (3-6 months) for the bone to fuse with the implant.
  • Crown or bridge attachment to complete the restoration.

Benefits

  • Permanent solution that can last a lifetime with proper care.
  • Natural look and feel – virtually indistinguishable from natural teeth.
  • Preserves jawbone and prevents bone loss over time.
  • No damage to adjacent teeth unlike traditional bridges.

Invisalign

Invisalign (invisible braces) uses a series of transparent, removable orthodontic aligners to gradually move your teeth into the desired position. Each set of custom-made aligners is worn for about two weeks before progressing to the next set in the treatment plan.

Treatment Process

  • Digital scan & 3D treatment plan to visualise your smile transformation.
  • Custom aligner fabrication based on your unique dental anatomy.
  • Wear each set of aligners for 2 weeks, removing only for eating and brushing.
  • Regular check-ups every 6-8 weeks to monitor progress and receive new aligners.

Benefits

  • Nearly invisible appearance – most people won't even notice you're wearing them.
  • Removable for eating, brushing, and flossing; no dietary restrictions.
  • More comfortable than metal braces with no wires or brackets to irritate.
  • Fewer dental visits needed compared to traditional braces.

Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment (endodontic therapy) eliminates infection that has reached the tooth nerve (pulp). The procedure involves removing the infected pulp, thoroughly cleaning and disinfecting the root canals, and sealing them to prevent reinfection.

Treatment Steps

  • Diagnosis & X-ray to assess the extent of infection and root canal anatomy.
  • Local anesthesia and tooth isolation with a rubber dam for comfort.
  • Removal of infected pulp tissue from the pulp chamber and root canals.
  • Cleaning and shaping of root canals using specialised instruments.
  • Filling with gutta-percha to seal the canals and prevent reinfection.
  • Crown placement for protection and restoration of full function.

Benefits

  • Saves your natural tooth, avoiding extraction whenever possible.
  • Eliminates pain and discomfort caused by infection or inflammation.
  • Prevents the spread of infection to surrounding teeth and jawbone.
  • Restores normal chewing function with a fully protected tooth.

Scaling and Polishing

Professional dental cleaning involves the removal of plaque, tartar (calculus), and stains from the surfaces of your teeth. Scaling removes hardened deposits above and below the gumline, while polishing smooths the tooth surfaces for a brighter, cleaner finish.

Benefits

  • Prevents gum disease by removing bacteria-harbouring deposits.
  • Prevents tooth decay and cavities formation.
  • Fresher breath by eliminating odour-causing bacteria.
  • Brighter, cleaner smile with stain removal.
  • Early detection of dental issues during routine examination.

Recommended Frequency

  • Every 6 months for optimal oral health maintenance.

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Wisdom tooth extraction is a surgical procedure to remove one or more wisdom teeth (third molars) that have become impacted, partially erupted, or are causing problems such as pain, infection, crowding, or damage to adjacent teeth.

Types of Extraction

  • Simple extraction – For fully erupted wisdom teeth that are easily accessible.
  • Surgical extraction – For impacted wisdom teeth that are trapped beneath the gum line or bone.

Process

  • X-ray assessment to evaluate the position and condition of the wisdom teeth.
  • Local anesthesia or sedation to ensure a comfortable procedure.
  • Extraction procedure performed by our experienced dental surgeon.
  • Post-operative care instructions provided for optimal healing.

Recovery

  • Recovery usually takes 3-7 days. We provide comprehensive aftercare instructions to ensure a smooth healing process.

Crowns & Bridges

Crowns and bridges are restorative dental solutions designed to repair damaged teeth or replace missing teeth. A crown (cap) covers and protects a damaged tooth, while a bridge spans the gap created by one or more missing teeth, anchored to adjacent teeth or implants.

Types

  • Porcelain / ceramic crowns – Natural-looking, metal-free restorations ideal for front teeth.
  • Metal-ceramic crowns – Strong, durable crowns with a porcelain exterior and metal substructure.
  • Dental bridges – Fixed prosthetic devices that replace missing teeth by bridging the gap.

Process

  • Tooth preparation – reshaping the tooth to accommodate the crown.
  • Impression taking for precise fabrication of your restoration.
  • Temporary crown placement to protect the prepared tooth.
  • Permanent crown fitting and cementation after laboratory fabrication.

Pediatric Dentistry

Children's dental care from infancy through adolescence, focusing on preventive care, early detection of dental issues, and building positive dental habits for a lifetime of healthy smiles. Our team creates a welcoming, child-friendly environment to make every visit enjoyable.

Services for Children

  • Regular check-ups – Age-appropriate examinations to monitor dental development.
  • Fluoride treatment – Strengthens enamel and prevents cavities.
  • Dental sealants – Protective coatings applied to the chewing surfaces of molars.
  • Fillings – Tooth-coloured restorations for treating cavities in baby and adult teeth.
  • Habit counselling – Guidance on thumb sucking, pacifier use, and other oral habits.

First Visit

  • Recommended by age 1 or within 6 months of the first tooth erupting, to establish a dental home and start preventive care early.

Periodontal Treatment

Periodontal treatment addresses gum diseases ranging from mild gingivitis (gum inflammation) to advanced periodontitis (gum disease with bone loss). Early intervention is key to preventing tooth loss and maintaining overall oral health.

Services

  • Scaling & root planing – Deep cleaning of tooth roots to remove plaque and tartar below the gumline.
  • Periodontal maintenance – Ongoing care to manage gum health after initial treatment.
  • Gum graft surgery – Repairing receding gums to protect tooth roots and improve aesthetics.
  • Pocket reduction surgery – Reducing deep gum pockets to facilitate better cleaning and healing.

Prevention

  • Brush your teeth twice daily with fluoride toothpaste.
  • Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth and along the gumline.
  • Visit your dentist regularly for professional cleanings and check-ups.

Oral Surgery

Oral surgery encompasses a range of surgical procedures performed in and around the mouth, jaw, and facial areas to treat various conditions. All procedures are performed with your comfort and safety as the highest priority.

Procedures

  • Wisdom tooth extraction – Removal of impacted or problematic wisdom teeth.
  • Dental implant placement – Surgical placement of titanium implant posts.
  • Jaw surgery – Corrective procedures for misaligned jaws or TMJ disorders.
  • Biopsy – Removal and examination of abnormal oral tissues.
  • Treatment of facial trauma – Management of injuries to the mouth, teeth, and facial structures.

Anaesthesia & Sedation

  • All oral surgery procedures are performed under local anesthesia with sedation options available for patients who experience anxiety or require more complex procedures.
