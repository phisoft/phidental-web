FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Phi Dental offers Braces, Dentures, Dental Implants, Invisalign, Root Canal Treatment, Scaling and Polishing, Teeth Whitening, Wisdom Tooth Extraction, Crowns & Bridges, Paediatric Dentistry, Periodontal Treatment, and Oral Surgery.
Walk-in patients are always welcome. However, we recommend booking in advance to minimise your waiting time. Same-day appointments are often available — simply give us a call.
We accept cash, credit and debit cards, and major insurance plans. Flexible payment arrangements are available for more extensive treatments — please discuss with our team.
Yes! All three Phi Dental branches are open 365 days a year — including all public holidays — from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
Our team is multilingual — proficient in English, Mandarin (Chinese), and Bahasa Malaysia. We ensure every patient is understood and feels comfortable throughout their treatment.
We recommend a check-up and professional cleaning (scaling & polishing) every six months. Regular visits help detect problems early and keep your teeth and gums healthy.
