Emergency Care

Contact us immediately at +6 013-4103103. We offer same-day emergency appointments to address urgent dental issues promptly. For after-hours emergencies, please call our emergency line.
Dental emergencies include:
• Severe toothache that does not subside
• Knocked-out tooth (time is critical — act fast!)
• Broken or cracked tooth
• Dental abscess (infection with swelling or pus)
• Uncontrolled bleeding from the mouth
If you are experiencing any of these, call us right away.
